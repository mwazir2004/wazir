About

Ines Varga is a fictional landscape photographer. Every image and video on this site was generated by a script, so the media is unique, free to reuse and safe for testing.

I carry one body and two lenses on longer walks. A wide lens for the foreground and a short telephoto for compressing the ridgelines into flat bands of tone.

Ridgeline Field Notes is a test site with generated images and videos. Nothing here is a real photograph.
